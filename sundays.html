Sundays

Sunday at 10:00 AM

We gather once a week, in person, at 7906 Nolensville Road, Arrington, TN 37014. There is no Saturday service and no online campus. If you can come, come. If you cannot, you are not behind.

The shape of the morning

People begin to arrive around 9:45. Worship starts at 10:00 AM and lasts about an hour and ten minutes. We sing hymns and a few newer songs from the piano. We pray. We read a passage out loud. Anita (or a guest) teaches for about twenty-five minutes. Then we go.

Communion

On the first Sunday of each month we share the Lord’s Table. The bread and cup are offered to anyone who wants to remember Jesus. Children may come with a parent. If you would rather stay in the pew, stay.

After church

Coffee is on the lawn when the weather is kind, and in the back room when it is not. Stay if you have time. There is no program you will miss by leaving.

This month

Walking Through the Psalms

Sundays in September · 10:00 AM

We are reading one psalm each Sunday and talking about what it means for an ordinary week. No homework. No fill-in-the-blank book. Just Scripture, a few songs, and a short teaching. You can start any Sunday.
