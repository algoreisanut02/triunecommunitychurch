I’m New

Plan a visit

Sunday is at 10:00 AM. The address is 7906 Nolensville Road, Arrington, TN 37014. You do not need to sign up. The note at the bottom is only if it would help you to tell us you are coming.

How to find the building

We meet at 7906 Nolensville Road, Arrington, TN 37014, in the little community of Triune. The church sits on Nolensville Road (US 31A / 41A), a few minutes north of I-840. Look for the red brick church with a tall white steeple, a green lawn out front, and a cross in the window above the front doors. If you are driving north from College Grove, we are on your right. If you are coming south from Nolensville, we are on your left. Turn in from the road. There is no gate.

Parking

Park along the side of the building, not on the front lawn. You can park close to the side doors and the ramp. Blue signs mark the shorter-walk spots. Save those if someone with you needs them, but no one will scold you for using them. Watch for the highway when you pull out — Nolensville Road moves fast.

Entrance

The front doors are white, at the top of the stairs, under the steeple. A cross is in the window above them. If stairs are hard, take the white ramp to the side door. Inside there is a small table with bulletins and water. Pick one up if you like, or walk straight in. We start gathering around 9:45.

Kids

Bring them. They may sit with you the whole time. During the sermon, Marcus hosts a class in the kids room down the hall on the left for younger children. You may walk them there and stay. You may also keep them in the pew. We do not have a check-in desk or name tags. We will find you if your child needs you.

Dress

Come as you are. Jeans are fine. A dress is fine. Work clothes are fine. This is a country church, not a fashion room. Wear what lets you sit still for an hour.

Service length

Sunday worship begins at 10:00 AM and lasts about an hour and ten minutes. We sing, pray, read Scripture, and hear a short teaching. Communion is on the first Sunday of the month. You may take it or let it pass. Both are welcome.

Accessibility

The front doors have stairs. A ramp with a white rail leads to the side door. Handicap parking is marked in blue near that entrance. There is space in the sanctuary for a wheelchair or stroller. The restroom is on the same floor. If you would like a large-print bulletin, ask James at the table.

Sit wherever you like

There are no reserved rows. Sit near the back, near the front, or on the end of a pew. You will not be asked to stand and introduce yourself. You will not be handed a microphone. After the blessing, people talk on the lawn. You can join them or go home. We mean that.

You will not be singled out. We will not put a spotlight on guests or ask you to fill out a card in the aisle.

I’m coming this Sunday

This is optional. It just helps us put out an extra chair and know your name. You are welcome either way.

I’m coming this Sunday

Glad you’ll be with us. Look for the brick church with the white steeple on Nolensville Road. Sit wherever you like. No one will call you out.

You can also reach us at info@triunecommunitychurch.com.
