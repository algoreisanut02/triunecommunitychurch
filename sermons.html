These notes are public. You do not need an account. Recordings are posted here when we have a clear one; until then, the notes are the sermon in short form.

September 6, 2026 · Walking Through the Psalms

The Lord is my shepherd

Psalm 23 · Anita

David names God as a shepherd who knows the path, the dark valley, and the table. We talked about what it means to be led, not driven, in an ordinary week in Arrington.

August 30, 2026 · Walking Through the Psalms

Help from the hills

Psalm 121 · Anita

The psalm does not pretend the road is easy. It says the Lord keeps you. We sat with that promise for people who are tired, caring for others, or starting over.

A life rooted like a tree by water looks quiet from the road. We asked what it means to delight in Scripture without turning it into a scorecard.

Luke 10:25–37 · Anita

Jesus tells a story about a man who stopped. We thought about Nolensville Road, and how mercy often looks like interrupting our own plans.

Isaiah 40:28–31 · James

Waiting on the Lord is not the same as doing nothing. James spoke from a long life of showing up, keeping the grounds, and trusting God with what we cannot fix.

The first invitation in John is not a speech. It is “come and see.” That is still our invitation. Come once. Sit down. See if this is a place you can breathe.
