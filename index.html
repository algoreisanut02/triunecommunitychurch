This Sunday

Sunday at 10:00 AM

Typical length

Worship lasts about an hour and ten minutes. We start on time and end without a long program after.

Parking

Park beside the building at 7906 Nolensville Road, Arrington, TN 37014. Marked spots near the ramp are for anyone who needs a shorter walk.

Kids

Children are welcome in the service. During the sermon there is also a class for little ones if that helps. You will not be asked to drop them off.

What a first Sunday feels like

Come as you are. You will not be singled out.

Parking

Turn in from Nolensville Road and park along the side of the building. Walk to the white front doors under the steeple, or take the ramp to the side door if stairs are hard.

Dress

Wear what you wear to the grocery store, or dress up if you like. Nobody is checking.

Kids

Bring them. Wiggles are fine. The kids room is down the hall on the left if you want it. You may stay with them.

In the room

Sit wherever you like. We will not ask visitors to stand, raise a hand, or wear a sticker. After church, people linger on the lawn with coffee. You can join them or go to your car. Both are fine.

Who we are

A simple church on this road

Triune Community Church is a neighborhood church on Nolensville Road in Arrington. We are not a denomination. We gather around Jesus, the Bible, and the people who live here. On Sunday we sing, pray, and open Scripture together. We try to be a place where you can come as you are, sit where you like, and not feel sold to. Kids are welcome. Questions are welcome. You do not have to believe everything we believe to spend a morning with us. If you live nearby and have been looking for a simple church, we would be glad to meet you.

This month

Walking Through the Psalms

Sundays in September · 10:00 AM

We are reading one psalm each Sunday and talking about what it means for an ordinary week. No homework. No fill-in-the-blank book. Just Scripture, a few songs, and a short teaching. You can start any Sunday.
